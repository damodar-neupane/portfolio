Background & Journey

Dedicated to Rigorous Evidence & Societal Progress

I am a results-driven MEAL (Monitoring, Evaluation, Accountability and Learning) professional with over 10 years of experience designing and leading multi-thematic education and health programs in Nepal.

My professional journey centers on foundational literacy, numeracy, and holistic early childhood development. I design and oversee rigorous baseline, midline, and endline evaluations utilizing standardized global frameworks such as CREDI and IDELA, alongside Nepal's national learning assessment tools (NARN).

Beyond technical M&E, I am deeply passionate about knowledge management, policy advisory, and philosophical reflection. Holding a Master's degree in Sociology from Tri-Chandra College, Tribhuvan University, my research and writing examine the intersection of Eastern philosophy, technology, and modern social dynamics in Nepal.
